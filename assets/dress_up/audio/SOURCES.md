# Original wardrobe audio

`wardrobe_waltz.wav` is a deterministic original stereo music-box composition,
generated by `tool/generate_dress_music.py`. 76 BPM, 16 bars in 3/4, 22,050 Hz,
16-bit PCM; decay and echo wrap around the loop. No third-party samples.

The wardrobe also uses the existing ColorHug short interaction sounds and the
device's installed Chinese speech voice. Music plays quietly and pauses on
backgrounding, navigation to other games, or mute. Narration can be interrupted
by the next choice. No microphone or camera permission is required for doll
photos: they are rendered by SceneKit and saved inside the app.
